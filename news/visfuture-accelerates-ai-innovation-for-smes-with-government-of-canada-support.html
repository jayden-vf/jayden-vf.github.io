VisFuture Accelerates AI Innovation for SMEs with Government of Canada Support

VisFuture receives $200,000 from FedDev Ontario through the Regional AI Initiative to commercialize its AI business insight platform for Canadian SMEs.

June 20, 2026·2 min read·By VisFuture

MARKHAM, ONTARIO — VisFuture Inc. today announced support from the Government of Canada through the Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario (FedDev Ontario). The $200,000 investment, provided through the Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative (RAII), will help VisFuture develop and commercialize its AI-enabled business insight platform, helping Canadian small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) make faster, better-informed decisions.

Making business data accessible through natural language

VisFuture’s AI Business Insight solution uses natural-language AI to give business users immediate access to real-time information across sales, inventory, finance and operations. Instead of waiting for technical teams to build reports or run queries, managers and staff can ask questions in plain language and get answers drawn from their own business systems — reducing reliance on technical resources and shortening the path from question to decision.

Key capabilities include:

  • Natural-language business queries and analytics
  • Real-time insights across enterprise data sources
  • AI-assisted dashboards and reporting
  • Automated business data integration
  • Faster and more informed decision-making

Supporting SME competitiveness and Canada’s digital economy

The project is expected to support AI innovation, strengthen the competitiveness of Canadian SMEs and contribute to Canada’s digital economy. The project runs from April 1, 2025, to March 31, 2027.

The Honourable Evan Solomon, Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation and Minister responsible for FedDev Ontario, stated that artificial intelligence is a key driver of Canada’s economic future, and that this investment helps Canadian businesses scale, supports Canada’s digital sovereignty and creates good-paying jobs.

Stephen Chan, Founder and CEO of VisFuture, said that FedDev Ontario’s support helps VisFuture accelerate commercialization and deliver practical, AI-driven business insight solutions for Canadian SMEs.

Learn more about VisFuture’s work on our projects page.

About VisFuture Inc.

Founded in 2021, VisFuture Inc. is a Markham, Ontario-based IT solutions provider delivering custom enterprise systems and AI platforms. The company builds custom software, AI solutions and IT services for Canadian SMEs and healthcare organizations.

About FedDev Ontario

Since 2009, the Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario (FedDev Ontario) has worked to advance and diversify the southern Ontario economy through funding and business services that support innovation, growth and job creation.
