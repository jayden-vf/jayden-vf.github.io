Work Order Management System

One shared platform for primary contractors and subcontractors to assign, track, document and bill work — with full visibility from start to finish.

Request a demo Core features

Overview

Work Order Management

When a primary contractor outsources tasks to subcontractors, information can quickly scatter across emails, spreadsheets and phone calls. A work order management system gives every party a single, shared platform to receive assignments, communicate, record progress and bill for completed work.

The VisFuture Work Order Management System centralizes communication and provides real-time visibility into every order. Engineering and construction companies can keep complete records, track subcontractor performance, and maintain compliance and quality control with documented inspections and sign-offs. Integrated scheduling, asset management and billing connect field activity with the back office.

VisFuture builds and configures the platform for contractors across Ontario and Canada, adapting forms, workflows and reports to how your projects actually run. Optional AI features, from assisted scheduling to automatic summaries and document extraction, help project managers spend less time chasing updates and more time delivering quality work.

Who it’s for

  • Engineering and construction firms
  • General and primary contractors
  • Subcontractors and trade partners
  • Facility maintenance and field service companies

Core features

Everything you need, in one place

  • Collaborative platformPrimary contractors and subcontractors share one workspace for assigning work orders, updating status and exchanging documents.
  • Record-keepingStore drawings, photos, inspection results, sign-offs and change history against each work order for a complete audit trail.
  • Secure messagingKeep project communication in context and on the record, instead of scattered across emails, texts and phone calls.
  • Performance trackingMeasure on-time completion, rework and quality results by subcontractor, project or work type.
  • Billing and paymentsTurn completed and approved work into invoices, track payment status and reconcile costs against each order.
  • Asset managementLink work orders to equipment, sites and assets so maintenance and service history is always available.
  • SchedulingPlan and assign work based on availability, priority and location, and adjust quickly when plans change.

AI-ready

AI that keeps projects on track

Our approach to AI

  • AI-assisted scheduling suggests crews and timing based on workload, location and past performance.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is a work order management system?

A work order management system is software that creates, assigns, tracks and documents work orders from request to completion and billing, giving everyone involved a shared view of the job.

How does it help when we outsource work to subcontractors?

The platform gives primary contractors and subcontractors one place to communicate, share documents, update progress and submit completed work, so there is real-time visibility and a full record of every order.

Can it help with compliance and quality control?

Yes. Detailed records, inspection results, sign-offs and change history are stored with each work order, helping engineering companies document quality and support their compliance requirements.

Can the system be customized for our industry?

Yes. VisFuture configures work order types, forms, approval steps and reports around your processes and can integrate the system with your ERP or accounting software.
