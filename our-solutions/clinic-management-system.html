Clinic Management System

One secure platform to run your clinic — from booking and charting to billing, virtual visits and reporting — with AI that takes routine work off your team’s plate.

Request a demo Core features

Overview

Clinic Management

Running a clinic means juggling schedules, charts, billing and patient communication — often across disconnected tools. The VisFuture Clinic Management System brings these workflows into one secure, cloud-based platform designed around how Canadian clinics actually operate.

Front-desk staff manage bookings and check-ins, providers chart in structured electronic health records, and managers see performance in real time — all from the same patient record. Virtual consultations, case management and integrated billing mean less duplicate entry and fewer errors.

Because every clinic is different, VisFuture tailors the system to your specialties, appointment types and reporting needs, and supports you after launch with maintenance and training.

Who it’s for

  • Family practices and walk-in clinics
  • Physiotherapy, rehabilitation and wellness clinics
  • Specialist and multi-disciplinary practices
  • Multi-location clinic groups

Core features

Everything you need, in one place

  • Patient managementKeep demographics, contacts, consents and visit history in a single, searchable patient profile your whole team can rely on.
  • Appointment schedulingManage multi-provider calendars, room bookings, waitlists and automated reminders that reduce no-shows.
  • Virtual consultationsRun secure video visits from the same system you use for in-person care, with notes and follow-ups captured in the chart.
  • Case managementTrack care plans, referrals and tasks across providers so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Electronic health records (EHR)Structured, customizable charting with templates, attachments and a complete audit trail.
  • Billing and financial managementGenerate invoices, record payments and reconcile accounts without re-entering data from the schedule or chart.
  • Reporting and analyticsSee appointment volumes, provider utilization, revenue and outstanding balances on live dashboards.
  • AI chatbotAnswer common patient questions and help patients book or reschedule around the clock.

AI-ready

AI built into daily clinic work

Our approach to AI

  • A patient-facing AI chatbot answers routine questions about hours, services and preparation, and routes complex requests to staff.

  • Natural-language queries let managers ask questions like “How many no-shows did we have last month?” without building reports.

  • Smart reminders and scheduling suggestions help fill gaps and reduce missed appointments.

  • AI features are optional and configurable, so your clinic decides where automation fits.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

Can the clinic management system be customized for our type of practice?

Yes. VisFuture builds and configures the system around your workflows, including charting templates, appointment types, roles and reports, rather than forcing you into a fixed process.

Does it connect to a patient portal?

Yes. The VisFuture Patient Portal is part of the same platform, so patients can book appointments, view records, message the clinic and pay bills securely.

Can it integrate with Ontario health systems?

VisFuture also delivers healthcare system integration, including connections with Ontario’s Provincial Client Registry, so patient demographic data can stay consistent across systems.

How is patient information protected?

The platform uses role-based access, encryption and audit logging, and our team works with you to align configuration and hosting with your privacy obligations.
