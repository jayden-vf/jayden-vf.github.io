Overview

Fleet Management

Managing vehicles, drivers and jobs across spreadsheets, phone calls and paper logs makes it hard to know where your fleet is and what it really costs. A fleet management system brings vehicle tracking, dispatch, routing, maintenance, fuel and compliance into one platform so you can run a safer, more efficient operation.

The VisFuture Fleet Management System gives dispatchers live visibility of every vehicle, helps plan optimized routes and keeps maintenance on schedule. Driver records, inspection documents and fuel data are organized and easy to report on, while asset utilization insights show whether your fleet is the right size for the work you do.

VisFuture tailors the system to fleets across Ontario and Canada, from small service companies to larger operations, and can connect it with your work order, ERP or accounting software. Add AI-powered predictive maintenance, smarter dispatch suggestions and natural-language reporting to turn fleet data into better day-to-day decisions.

Who it’s for

  • Delivery, courier and logistics companies
  • Field service and maintenance contractors
  • Construction and engineering firms with vehicle fleets
  • Municipal and public-sector fleets
  • Healthcare and home care organizations with mobile staff

Core features

Everything you need, in one place

  • Vehicle location trackingSee the live location and status of every vehicle on a map, with trip history for review and reporting.
  • Driver managementMaintain driver profiles, licences, assignments and hours, and keep training and documentation in one place.
  • Dispatch and schedulingAssign jobs to the right driver and vehicle, communicate changes instantly and keep customers updated.
  • Route optimizationPlan efficient routes that account for stops, time windows and distance to reduce driving time and fuel use.
  • Maintenance managementSchedule preventive maintenance by date or distance, log repairs and track service history for each vehicle.
  • Fuel managementRecord fuel purchases and consumption to spot unusual usage and understand fuel costs per vehicle or route.
  • ComplianceKeep inspection records, vehicle documents and driver credentials organized and up to date, with reminders before they expire.
  • Asset utilizationMeasure how often vehicles and equipment are used to right-size your fleet and redeploy idle assets.

AI-ready

AI that makes every kilometre count

Our approach to AI

  • Predictive maintenance uses vehicle history and usage patterns to flag service needs before breakdowns happen.

  • AI-assisted route and dispatch suggestions adapt to new jobs, traffic and delays during the day.

  • Anomaly detection highlights unusual fuel use, idle time or driving patterns for review.

  • Natural-language queries answer questions like “Which vehicles are due for service next month?” without building reports.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is a fleet management system?

A fleet management system is software that helps organizations track vehicles, manage drivers, dispatch work, plan routes, schedule maintenance and control fuel and compliance costs from one place.

How does fleet management software reduce costs?

By optimizing routes, tracking fuel use, scheduling preventive maintenance and improving vehicle utilization, fleet software helps reduce wasted time, fuel and unplanned repairs.

Can the system integrate with our existing GPS devices or business software?

In many cases, yes. VisFuture reviews your current telematics devices and business systems, such as ERP, accounting or work order software, and builds the integrations your fleet needs.

Is the fleet management system suitable for small fleets?

Yes. VisFuture configures the system to the size and needs of your operation, so small and growing fleets can start with core features and add more over time.
