Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Manage every customer relationship — from first lead to repeat sale and support — in a CRM connected to the rest of your business.

Request a demo Core features

Overview

CRM

Customer information often lives in inboxes, spreadsheets and the memories of individual employees. CRM software for small business brings it together, giving your team a shared view of every contact, conversation, opportunity and support request.

The VisFuture CRM goes beyond a contact list. It connects lead management, sales pipelines, marketing automation and customer service with the rest of your operations, including accounting, client management and warehouse management. Customizable workflows automate routine tasks, while dashboards and reports show how your pipeline, campaigns and service teams are performing.

VisFuture configures the CRM to match how your Canadian business sells and serves customers, and integrates it with the systems you already use. Add AI features such as lead scoring, AI-drafted replies, a website chatbot and natural-language insights to help your team spend less time on data entry and more time building relationships.

Who it’s for

  • Small and mid-sized businesses
  • Professional service firms
  • Distributors and wholesalers with account-based sales
  • Service and support teams

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is CRM software?

Customer relationship management (CRM) software helps businesses organize contacts, track interactions, manage sales and marketing, and deliver customer service from one shared system.

Why does a small business need a CRM?

A CRM replaces scattered spreadsheets, inboxes and notes, helping small teams follow up consistently, understand their customers and grow sales without losing track of relationships.

Can the CRM integrate with our accounting or ERP system?

Yes. VisFuture integrates the CRM with accounting, client management, warehouse management, ERP and other business applications so information flows between them.

Can workflows be customized?

Yes. Workflows, fields, pipelines and reports are configured around your processes, and VisFuture can build custom features when needed.
