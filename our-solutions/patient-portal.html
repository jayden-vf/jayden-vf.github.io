Overview

Patient Portal

Patients expect the same convenience from their clinic that they get from banking or shopping online. The VisFuture Patient Portal gives them a secure, easy-to-use place to book appointments, view their health records, request prescription renewals, message the care team and pay bills — on any device, at any time.

Because the portal is built into the VisFuture Clinic Management System, every booking, message and payment lands directly in your clinic’s workflow. Staff spend less time on the phone, providers receive organized requests, and patients get faster answers. Personalized reminders and curated health education resources help patients prepare for visits and follow through on care plans.

VisFuture designs the patient portal for clinics across Ontario and Canada, configuring what patients can see and do to match your services and privacy policies. Optional AI features, such as a patient-facing chatbot and smart reminders, can be switched on as your clinic is ready.

Who it’s for

  • Family practices and walk-in clinics
  • Specialist and multi-disciplinary clinics
  • Physiotherapy, rehabilitation and wellness clinics
  • Clinics offering virtual care
  • Multi-location clinic groups

Core features

Everything you need, in one place

  • Personal health records accessPatients can view visit summaries, test results and documents your clinic chooses to share, in one secure place.
  • Virtual consultation schedulingPatients book video visits online and receive the link and instructions automatically, with the appointment synced to your clinic calendar.
  • Prescription managementPatients review current medications and submit renewal requests that arrive in the provider’s queue for review.
  • Secure messagingEncrypted two-way messaging replaces phone tag and unsecured email for non-urgent questions, with every exchange saved to the record.
  • Appointment managementPatients book, reschedule or cancel in-person visits based on real availability, freeing up the front desk.
  • Billing and paymentsPatients see statements and outstanding balances and pay online, with payments posted back to your clinic’s billing system.
  • Health education resourcesShare condition-specific guides, preparation instructions and aftercare information curated by your clinicians.
  • Personalized health remindersAutomated reminders for appointments, follow-ups, screenings and medication renewals keep patients on track.
  • Cross-device compatibilityA responsive, accessible design works on phones, tablets and desktops without requiring an app download.

AI-ready

AI that helps patients help themselves

Our approach to AI

  • An integrated AI chatbot answers common questions about bookings, preparation and portal use, and hands off to staff when a human is needed.

  • Smart reminders adapt timing and content to each patient’s appointments, prescriptions and follow-up plans.

  • AI-assisted message triage can sort incoming patient messages by topic so the right team member responds first.

  • Every AI feature is optional and configurable, and clinical decisions always remain with your providers.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is a patient portal?

A patient portal is a secure website where patients can view their health information, book appointments, message their care team, request prescription renewals and pay bills online.

Does the patient portal work with the VisFuture Clinic Management System?

Yes. The portal is part of the same platform, so appointments, messages, prescriptions and payments flow directly into your clinic’s records.

Do patients need to install an app?

No. The portal runs in any modern web browser and adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

How is patient data protected in the portal?

The portal uses secure sign-in, encryption, role-based access and audit logging, and VisFuture works with your clinic to configure it in line with your PHIPA and PIPEDA obligations.
