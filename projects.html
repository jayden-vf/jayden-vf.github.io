AI Project

AI-based Business Insight project

An AI-enabled tool that connects to ERP and business systems to deliver real-time data, automated reports and predictive insights — as a stand-alone product or an ERP add-on, for Canadian and international markets.

Core features

What the platform does

Ask questions in plain language and get answers from ERP, CRM, HRMS and finance data — no SQL or report building required.

Spot trends and forecast demand, sales and operational risks using AI models trained on your business data.

Generate recurring reports and AI-assisted dashboards automatically, so teams spend time on decisions instead of spreadsheets.

Connect securely to the systems you already run, with automated data integration across sources.

A clean, conversational interface on desktop and mobile that anyone in the business can use.

In detail

Five capabilities, one goal: give every team in a small or mid-sized business fast, trustworthy answers from the data it already has.

01

Natural-language queries

Anyone can ask a question the way they would ask a colleague. The assistant works out which systems hold the answer, queries them, and replies in plain language, with a chart or table and the sources it used.

  • Answers drawn from ERP, CRM, HRMS and finance data
  • Every answer shows the systems it came from
  • Follow-up questions keep the context of the conversation
  • No SQL, report builders or analyst queue

Predictive analytics

AI models learn from your own sales, inventory and financial history to forecast what comes next, and flag the risks and opportunities worth acting on before they show up in month-end numbers.

  • Demand, sales and cash flow forecasts
  • Early warnings for stock-outs and slow-paying accounts
  • Anomaly detection on margins and order volumes
  • Forecasts refresh automatically as new data arrives

03

Reporting automation

Recurring reports build themselves. Schedule them once and the platform pulls fresh data, writes an AI summary of what changed, and delivers charts and a PDF to the right people.

  • Daily, weekly and month-end schedules
  • AI-written summaries highlight what changed and why
  • Delivery by email, team channel or report library
  • AI-assisted dashboards for each team

Enterprise integration

The platform connects securely to the systems you already run, as a stand-alone product or an add-on to your ERP, and keeps data in sync automatically so answers are always current.

  • Connectors for ERP, CRM, accounting, HRMS and e-commerce
  • Spreadsheets and databases mapped in a few clicks
  • Role-based access respects existing permissions
  • Encrypted connections and audit logs

User-centric design
