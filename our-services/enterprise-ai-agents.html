Enterprise Knowledge Base & AI Agents

Give your team an AI that knows your business. We turn your documents and systems into a secure knowledge base and deploy AI agents that answer questions and handle routine work — running on your own infrastructure, so your data stays under your control.

Book a consultation

Overview

A private AI knowledge base and agents, deployed on infrastructure you control.

Every organization holds valuable knowledge in policies, procedures, contracts, manuals and years of email and support history. Yet finding the right answer often means searching shared drives or interrupting a colleague. VisFuture’s enterprise knowledge base and AI agents put that knowledge to work, so your team gets clear, sourced answers in seconds.

Unlike public AI tools, our solutions can be deployed locally: on your own servers, in a private cloud or in an isolated network. The models, search index and data stay under your control, which matters for organizations that handle client records, health information, financial data or confidential designs. Access follows your existing permissions, and every question and action is logged.

On top of the knowledge base, AI agents take on routine work such as drafting responses, preparing quotes, creating tickets, updating records in your ERP or CRM and compiling reports, with a person approving the steps that matter. We start with one team and a focused set of documents, prove the value with real users, then expand across your organization in the GTA and across Canada.

What we deliver

What’s included

01

Enterprise knowledge base

Bring policies, procedures, contracts, manuals, project files and support history into one searchable knowledge base. Staff ask questions in plain language and get answers that cite the exact document and section.

02

Local and private deployment

Run the language models, search index and application on your own servers, in a private cloud or in an isolated network. Documents and prompts stay inside your environment and are not sent to public AI services unless you decide otherwise.

03

AI agents for everyday work

Agents go beyond answering questions. They draft replies, fill in forms, create tickets, update ERP and CRM records and prepare reports, with a person approving any action that matters before it is carried out.

04

Connectors to your systems

Connect file shares, SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive, email, ERP, CRM, help desk and databases. Scheduled sync keeps the knowledge base current as documents change.

05

Permission-aware access and audit

Answers follow your existing user and group permissions, so people only see what they are already allowed to see. Every question, source and agent action is logged for review.

06

Model selection and sizing

Choose open-weight models that fit your hardware, languages and use cases, and size servers or cloud instances to your number of users — without paying for more capacity than you need.

07

Evaluation, monitoring and support

Test answer quality against real questions from your team before launch, then monitor accuracy, usage and performance, and keep models, connectors and security patches up to date.

How we work

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What does local or private deployment mean?

The language models, search index and application run on servers you own or in a private cloud environment dedicated to your organization. Your documents, questions and answers stay inside that environment rather than being processed by a public AI service.

Do we need special hardware?

It depends on the number of users, the size of your document collection and the models you choose. Some organizations start with a single GPU server or a private cloud GPU instance. We recommend a setup during discovery so you invest only in what you need.

How is this different from a website chatbot?

A website chatbot answers your customers using public information. An enterprise knowledge base serves your own staff with internal documents and data, respects their access permissions, and can use agents to complete tasks inside your business systems.

How do you keep answers accurate?

Answers are generated from your own documents and include citations, so staff can check the source. The assistant says when it cannot find an answer, low-confidence responses can be routed for review, and we test quality against real questions before launch.

Can it work in English and French?

Yes. Current language models handle both English and French, and staff can ask questions in either language across documents written in either language.
