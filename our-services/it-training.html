Overview

Hands-on AI, Power BI, ERP and cybersecurity courses for teams.

Technology only delivers value when people know how to use it well. VisFuture’s IT training programs close the gap between your team’s current skills and today’s technology demands, with tailored, hands-on sessions led by experienced professionals.

Our course catalogue reflects what organizations in Ontario and across Canada need most right now. AI courses — Introduction to AI and AI in Business — help staff understand modern AI and apply it safely and productively. Power BI training turns spreadsheets into interactive dashboards and real insight. ERP courses cover CRM, Supply Chain Management, Finance Management and HR and Payroll, while our Introduction to Cybersecurity and Comprehensive Cybersecurity courses strengthen your first line of defence.

Training is also a key part of successful AI adoption. When teams understand what AI can and cannot do, they use it with more confidence and better judgment. Courses are available online or in person, and we can design custom sessions around your systems, data and goals. Whether you are upskilling a small team in the GTA or preparing staff for a new platform rollout, VisFuture helps your people get the most from your technology.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

Which IT training courses does VisFuture offer?

Our courses include Introduction to AI, AI in Business, Power BI, CRM in ERP, SCM in ERP, Finance Management in ERP, HR and Payroll in ERP, Introduction to Cybersecurity and Comprehensive Cybersecurity.

Are courses available online or in person?

Both. Courses can be delivered as live online sessions or in person, and we can run private sessions for your team.

Can training be customized for our company?

Yes. We tailor content, examples and exercises to your systems, data and industry, which is especially useful when rolling out new software or AI tools.

Do we need technical experience to take the AI courses?

No. Introduction to AI is designed for non-technical professionals and explains key concepts in plain language before moving to practical business applications.
