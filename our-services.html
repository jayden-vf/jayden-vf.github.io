01

AI Adoption & Integration

Move from AI curiosity to measurable results. We help you find the right use cases, prepare your data and build AI into the systems your team already uses — responsibly and step by step.

  • AI readiness assessmentReview your processes, systems, data and team skills to understand where your organization stands and what it needs before investing in AI.
  • Use-case discovery and prioritizationRun focused workshops to identify AI opportunities, then rank them by business value, feasibility and risk so you start where it matters most.
  • Data readiness and preparationAssess data quality, structure and access across your systems, and put the clean, connected foundations in place that reliable AI depends on.
  • AI integration into existing systemsAdd LLM assistants, chatbots, natural-language analytics, predictive models and document automation to your ERP, CRM or clinic software — no rip-and-replace required.
  • Responsible AI, governance and privacyDefine usage policies, human oversight, access controls and data-handling practices that align AI with your privacy obligations and business values.
  • Training and change managementEquip staff to use AI tools confidently and safely with hands-on training, clear guidelines and support through the transition.

02

Enterprise Knowledge Base & AI Agents

Give your team an AI that knows your business. We turn your documents and systems into a secure knowledge base and deploy AI agents that answer questions and handle routine work — running on your own infrastructure, so your data stays under your control.

  • Enterprise knowledge baseBring policies, procedures, contracts, manuals, project files and support history into one searchable knowledge base. Staff ask questions in plain language and get answers that cite the exact document and section.
  • Local and private deploymentRun the language models, search index and application on your own servers, in a private cloud or in an isolated network. Documents and prompts stay inside your environment and are not sent to public AI services unless you decide otherwise.
  • AI agents for everyday workAgents go beyond answering questions. They draft replies, fill in forms, create tickets, update ERP and CRM records and prepare reports, with a person approving any action that matters before it is carried out.
  • Connectors to your systemsConnect file shares, SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive, email, ERP, CRM, help desk and databases. Scheduled sync keeps the knowledge base current as documents change.
  • Permission-aware access and auditAnswers follow your existing user and group permissions, so people only see what they are already allowed to see. Every question, source and agent action is logged for review.
  • Model selection and sizingChoose open-weight models that fit your hardware, languages and use cases, and size servers or cloud instances to your number of users — without paying for more capacity than you need.
  • Evaluation, monitoring and supportTest answer quality against real questions from your team before launch, then monitor accuracy, usage and performance, and keep models, connectors and security patches up to date.

Cybersecurity Services

Robust, practical cybersecurity that protects your digital infrastructure, data and reputation from evolving threats.

  • Security risk assessmentIdentify your critical assets, likely threats and current gaps, with practical recommendations prioritized by risk and effort.
  • Vulnerability managementScan systems and applications regularly, prioritize findings and track remediation so known weaknesses get fixed.
  • Endpoint and network securityHarden devices, servers, firewalls and Wi-Fi, and deploy endpoint protection that uses behavioural and AI-assisted detection to flag suspicious activity.
  • Identity and access managementStrengthen sign-in with multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access and clean processes for onboarding and offboarding users.
  • Security awareness trainingTeach staff to recognize phishing, social engineering and unsafe practices, including emerging risks from AI-generated scams.
  • Incident response planningPrepare clear roles, procedures and communication plans so your team knows exactly what to do if an incident occurs.
