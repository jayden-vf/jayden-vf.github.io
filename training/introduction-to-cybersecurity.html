Introduction to Cybersecurity

Learn how cyber threats work and the practical steps that keep you, your data and your organization safe online.

Overview

About this course

Cyber threats affect everyone, from individuals managing personal accounts to organizations protecting sensitive data. Introduction to Cybersecurity is designed for learners with minimal or no prior experience who want a clear, practical foundation in how digital security works.

The course starts with the fundamentals — what cybersecurity is, how it has evolved and the main types of cyber threats — then covers network security basics, information security and data protection, threats and vulnerabilities, and the role of security policies and procedures. You will also look ahead at emerging technologies, anticipated threats and new innovations in cybersecurity solutions.

Most importantly, you will learn how cybersecurity affects your daily life and how to protect yourself using password managers, antivirus and anti-malware software, two-factor authentication, encryption tools, firewalls and secure browsing habits. A hands-on workshop lets you implement basic security measures and practise responding to simulated cyber threats. VisFuture delivers the course online or in person, including custom sessions for teams.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn cybersecurity with no IT experience?

Yes. This course is designed for beginners with minimal or no prior experience. Concepts are explained clearly and reinforced with practical, guided exercises.

What are the most common cyber threats covered?

The course covers common threats such as malware, phishing and network attacks, and shows how tools like password managers, two-factor authentication, encryption and firewalls help defend against them.

Can you deliver cybersecurity awareness training to our staff?

Yes. VisFuture delivers the course online or in person and can run private sessions for your organization, focused on the risks your team faces day to day.

How long is the course and how much does it cost?

Sessions are scheduled and quoted to fit your team’s needs. Contact VisFuture to discuss format, timing and pricing.
