Overview

About this course

Artificial intelligence is no longer just a technology topic — it is a business strategy. AI in Business connects AI concepts to practical, real-world use so leaders can drive innovation, improve decision-making and build a lasting competitive advantage.

The course explores how organizations apply AI across marketing and sales, customer service, supply chain management and day-to-day operations. You will learn how AI-driven automation streamlines workflows and removes repetitive work, and how predictive tools help anticipate market trends and maintenance needs before they become problems.

Real case studies — from manufacturing and predictive maintenance to trend analysis and AI chatbots — show what works, what does not and the lessons learned along the way. Interactive brainstorming sessions and group discussions then help you shape AI ideas for your own organization. VisFuture delivers the course online or in person, and can tailor private sessions to your team.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

How can AI help a small or mid-sized business?

AI can automate repetitive tasks, power customer service chatbots, improve marketing targeting, forecast demand and surface trends in your data. The course shows where these applications deliver value and how to get started.

Do I need a technical background to take this AI for business course?

No. The course focuses on strategy, use cases and decision-making rather than programming, so it suits managers, executives and entrepreneurs.

Can the course be customized for our company or industry?

Yes. VisFuture can deliver a private session for your team, online or in person, with case studies and brainstorming focused on your industry and business goals.

How long is the training and what does it cost?

Sessions are scheduled and quoted to fit your team’s needs. Contact VisFuture to discuss format, timing and pricing.
